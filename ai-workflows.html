Where the hours go

Agents lose hours to lead follow-up, scheduling, and paperwork. That is the work these workflows are for.

Lead follow-up

A new inquiry needs a first reply, then another check-in. It slips when someone is already out at a showing.

Scheduling

Showings and appointments take a string of messages just to find a time and keep it straight.

Paperwork

Checklists, documents, and transaction admin pile up between contract and close.

What I set up

These are workflows I can put in place. We choose which ones belong in your office.

Instant lead response and follow-up sequences

A reply when a lead comes in, then a follow-up sequence so the conversation does not depend on someone watching the inbox.

CRM updates

Notes, stage changes, and contact updates written into GoHighLevel or a similar CRM your team already uses.

Showing and appointment scheduling

Help booking showings and appointments, with the details kept together.

Document and transaction checklist prep

Checklists and document prep so a file is ready for the next step.

Listing marketing drafts

Draft listing descriptions and other marketing copy for an agent to review before anything goes out.

Tenant and maintenance request triage

For property managers: tenant and maintenance requests sorted so the urgent ones are easy to see.
