The hours

Where the time goes

These jobs show up on small teams in any industry. They are easy to postpone, and they pile up while the work clients actually see is still waiting.

  • Lead follow-up

    A new inquiry needs a first reply, then another check-in. It slips when everyone is already with a customer.

  • Scheduling

    Finding a time, confirming it, and changing it turns into a chain of messages for every appointment.

  • Inbox and paperwork

    Email, forms, and the documents between a request and a finished task sit in the same pile.

  • Data entry

    The same details get typed again into a CRM, a spreadsheet, or another system the team already uses.

  • Reporting

    Someone still has to gather what happened so the team can see it in one place.

The engagement

What an AI workflow build looks like

A build is a working setup for a real process. This is the shape of the work, from the first walkthrough to support after the team is using it.

  1. Map the process

    We walk through how the work actually moves: who does it, which tools it touches, and where it stalls.

  2. Build the automations and AI agents

    I set up the automations and AI agents on tools your team already uses. When the CRM is GoHighLevel, the workflow can live there.

  3. Train the team

    The people who will use it learn what it does, what they still review, and how to hand work to it.

  4. Support

    After the setup is in use, I stay available to adjust it as the team works with it.
