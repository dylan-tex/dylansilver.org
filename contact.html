Quick Questions?

How quickly can you help me find an apartment?

Response time depends on your timeline and requirements, but I typically can present initial options same-day. For urgent relocations, I prioritize your search accordingly.

Do you charge for apartment locating services?

Apartment locating services are completely free to you — I'm compensated by landlords and property management companies. For single family home rentals, there may be a fee equivalent to 1 month's rent if the landlord is unwilling to cover our fee.

What areas of Austin do you know best?

I have comprehensive knowledge of Austin neighborhoods across the entire metro area, from downtown to the suburbs. Let me know your preferences and I'll guide you to the best fit.

Can you help with short sales or foreclosures?

Yes! I've completed 20+ cash offer deals, many of which were facing foreclosure. I'm also Short Sale and Foreclosure Resource certified through NAR.

What's your experience with relocating professionals?

Extensive. I specialize in helping tech professionals, executives, and families relocate to Austin. I understand the unique challenges of moving to a new city and can guide you through the entire process.

Do you have international relocation experience?

Yes! I've lived abroad (Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic) for over 6 months, which gives me unique perspective on helping international professionals transition to Austin.
