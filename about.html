About Dylan Silver

Certified REALTOR®, thought leader, and Austin relocation expert

My Story

I'm a passionate real estate professional and entrepreneur dedicated to helping people find their perfect Austin home—whether that's a rental apartment, a dream house purchase, or a relocation success story.

My journey in real estate is driven by a genuine desire to make transitions smooth and stress-free. I combine deep market expertise with a personal touch that goes beyond typical real estate transactions. I understand that moving to a new city or buying a home is one of life's biggest decisions, and I'm honored to guide people through that process.

Beyond real estate, I'm a thought leader in real estate investing and business strategy. As host of the Straight to the Money Podcast and a regular contributor to Investor Fuel (400+ episodes), I share insights and perspectives that help professionals and investors make better financial decisions.

International Experience

I've lived abroad for over half a year in Santo Domingo, Dominican Republic, which gave me unique perspectives on adaptation, cultural integration, and what it really takes to thrive in a new environment. This experience deeply informs how I help others navigate relocations.

Personal Life

When I'm not helping clients find their perfect Austin home, you'll find me hitting the gym or hitting the pavement. I'm an avid weightlifter and runner who believes in maintaining a balanced, healthy lifestyle. This dedication to personal excellence translates into the meticulous attention I give every client relationship.
